FortiBoothDemo plays a chained set of Fortinet product demos full screen, with a short intro slide before each. You curate the playlist once; one click runs it at the booth all day.

5-sec intro slide per demo Vimeo + YouTube embeds, both work Export .json, carry to any booth laptop

Set it up in three steps.

01
Build

Paste a Fortinet URL

Open the FortiBoothDemo settings, paste any video.fortinet.com/… URL, click Fetch & add. The extension finds the video and generates an intro slide you can edit.

03
Launch

One click, full screen

Press ▶ Start demo loop. A new tab opens, goes full screen, plays the playlist end-to-end, then starts over. Space pauses, ←/→ skip, Esc leaves full screen.
